## General Documentation for rosetta package

The `rosetta` package provides a comprehensive framework for managing and interacting with complex data structures, particularly those related to legal agreements within a financial system. It encompasses a range of functionalities, including the definition of legal agreement types, terms, parties involved, and specific clauses. This structured representation allows for efficient handling and analysis of contractual relationships in a digital environment. The package also includes utilities for date and time manipulation, mathematical operations, and other fundamental data processing tasks.

The `base` module lays the groundwork for the entire package by defining essential data types and constants used throughout the system. It serves as a foundation for other modules within the `rosetta` package, ensuring consistency and reusability of core components. The `legaldocumentation-common-type` module focuses on modeling legal agreements, providing classes that represent various aspects of contracts, such as agreement types, terms, parties involved, and specific clauses. This structured representation enables efficient management and analysis of complex contractual relationships within a financial system.
